7. Multi-Axis Coordination
   What they’re testing
   Advanced motion capability
   Concepts
   Master-slave control
   Electronic gearing
   Interpolation (linear, circular)
   Example
   X-Y gantry must move synchronously
   Laser scan path must be precise
   Strong answer

“I use coordinated motion where one axis acts as master and others follow with defined ratios or trajectories. Synchronization is handled by the motion controller to ensure deterministic timing.”

What NOT to say
“I just start both motors at the same time”
